# Activity: Enhance the Figma Prototype

**IGME-110 | Week 10 | Started in class Tuesday, finished on your own**

We build a prototype together in class on Tuesday. Then you take it further on your own and hand it
in by Thursday night.

## Requirements

Start from what we made together and turn it into a **medium fidelity** design:

- **At least 3 interactive pages**, connected by buttons, links, or other interactions
- **At least 1 scrollable element**
- **Consistent visual design** across all pages, more polished than a wireframe

Then add at least one enhancement of your own. Pick any of these, or more than one:

- Another page opened by a button
- A scrollable area
- A modal popup
- An animation or transition

Make an honest effort to add something that's actually interesting and actually works.

While you're adding color, run the three checks from Tuesday: nothing that relies on color alone,
text with enough contrast to read easily, and buttons big enough to tap with a thumb.

## What to hand in

Hand it in on myCourses by Thursday night:

1. **Your Figma link.** Click **Share** (top right in Figma), set it to **Anyone with the link can
   view**, then open the link in a private browser window to check that it works.
2. **A sentence or two on what you added** and where to find it. For example: *"I added a Settings
   page with a scrolling list, opened from the gear icon in the footer."* If you don't say what you
   added, I can't grade it.

## AI Expectations

Do the build yourself. The point is getting comfortable in Figma before your team builds the
Project 3 hi-fi there. Figma's built-in AI features (generating a design or a screen for you) are
not allowed here, same as in Project 3. Asking a chatbot how to find a Figma setting is fine.

If you use AI for anything, add a line to your hand-in saying which tool and what you used it for.
