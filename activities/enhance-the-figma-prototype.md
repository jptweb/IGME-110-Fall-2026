# Activity: Enhance the Figma Prototype

**IGME-110 | Week 10B | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** Requirements below are lifted from Fall 2025. The class-build half needs
> rewriting once week 10 is planned.

We build a prototype together in class. Then you take it further on your own.

## Requirements

Start from what we made together and turn it into a **medium fidelity** design:

- **At least 3 interactive pages**, connected by buttons, links, or other interactions
- **At least 1 scrollable element**
- **Consistent visual design** across all pages, more polished than a wireframe

Then add at least one enhancement of your own. Pick any of these, or more than one:

- Another page opened by a button
- A scrollable area
- A modal popup
- An animation or transition

Make an honest effort to add something that's actually interesting and actually works.

## If you missed class

Read the week 10 notes and follow the build on your own before you start. The notes link
the teaching guide and a walkthrough video.
