# Activity: Using Our Slack Workspace

**IGME-110 | Week 1B | In-Class Activity**

We use Slack for course communication, project coordination, and getting help fast. This is a
one-time setup, done in class, so nobody is quietly locked out in week six.

## Why Slack and not Discord

Most of you have used Discord, or the free version of Slack. The basics transfer. RIT's
enterprise version adds a few things that matter:

- **One login for every RIT workspace**, using your normal RIT authentication
- You can find and message **anyone at RIT** by name or user ID

Three reasons we're not on Discord:

1. **Security and privacy.** Coursework is held to a standard that only enterprise tools meet.
2. **Industry uses Slack.** Businesses rarely run internal communication on Discord. Getting
   comfortable with the tools you'll actually encounter is part of the point.
3. **Boundaries.** When the same app is your social life and your work, both get worse. It's
   also a little awkward when your professor can see you're in a match instead of finishing a
   deliverable.

## Connecting

**Start here: https://rit.enterprise.slack.com/**

Getting to that exact URL is most of the battle. Once you're there, you'll see a big green
**Sign in with RIT login** button. Click it and you'll land on RIT's normal authentication
screen, the same one you see for everything else. Sign in with your username (`abc1234`) and
your RIT password.

> ⚠️ **You are not creating a Slack account.** There is no Slack username and no Slack
> password. If a screen is asking you to type an email address or make up a password, you're in
> the wrong place. Go back to the URL above and look for the green button.

You don't need to type `@rit.edu` or `@g.rit.edu` anywhere, which is the part that catches
people. Most Slack sign-in flows start by asking for an email, and ours doesn't work that way.
Your personal Google or Slack account won't get you in either.

Once you're through, that page lists every RIT workspace you belong to. Everyone is in RIT
Community by default, and you should see our course workspace as well.

**If you can't find or access our workspace, tell me right away** so I can add you. Don't wait
until you need something.

**You'll stay signed in for a good while**, so this is mostly a one-time hassle. If you do get
signed out, come back to the same URL and click the same green button.

## Workspaces and channels

The sidebar shows channels from **all** your workspaces at once. To narrow it, click
**All workspaces** and pick one.

Every channel in our workspace starts with `110`, so they group together in the sidebar even
when you're viewing everything.

You're added automatically to a few:

- **#110-announcements** for things from me. You can read it, you can't post in it.
- **#110-general** for questions about any part of the class. The 110 instructors and TAs all
  watch this one, so you'll usually get an answer faster here than by email.
- **#110-random** for everything else.
- **#110-inclass-activities** for posting your work when an activity asks you to share it.

Other channels exist that you have to join yourself: **Add Channels** at the bottom of the
channel list, or **Channels → Manage → Browse Channels**.

## What to do today

### 1. Read the rules

**[→ IGME-110 Slack Ground Rules](../documents/slack-rules.md)**, also pinned in
**#110-announcements**. React with 👍 on that pinned post to confirm you've read them.

### 2. Introduce yourself

Find and join **#110-introductions**, then post a short introduction with:

- Your major
- Where you're from
- One interesting thing you learned this week

Reply to a couple of other people's introductions if you like. **Use "Reply in thread"** so the
channel doesn't fill up with side conversations. If you don't, an instructor or TA will
probably tease you about it.

### 3. Add a profile picture

It genuinely helps us learn who you are. Click your profile icon at the bottom left, then
**Profile → Edit**, and upload an image. A photo of you is best, but an avatar is fine.

Don't impersonate anyone or post anything that violates the RIT Code of Conduct.

> **Your display name comes from the RIT directory** and can't be changed in Slack. To change
> it, go to start.rit.edu → Directory Information → Name Preferences and set your preferred
> name. That updates Slack, Zoom, myCourses, and class rosters together.

## Worth doing, not required today

### The phone app: I'd actually get this one

I'm generally not a fan of installing an app for something that already works fine as a
website. The Slack mobile app is the exception I make. It keeps you signed in, and it's the
fastest way to reach me or your group when something comes up the night before a deadline.

### The desktop app: up to you

I don't use it. It mirrors the web version almost exactly, so a browser tab does the same job.
If you'd rather have it in your dock, go ahead. Nothing in this course needs it.

### Connect Google Drive

You'll use Google Drive for group work this semester, and the Slack integration makes sharing
files much easier. Go to **Apps** in the sidebar, pick **Google Drive**, and click
**Configuration**. You'll authenticate with your RIT Google account. This only affects your
account, not anyone else's.

### Keeping Slack from becoming overwhelming

**Use threaded replies.** Less clutter, conversations stay grouped, and only people in the
thread get notified.

**Tune your notifications.** Preferences → Notifications. Slack's own help article on this is
good and worth ten minutes.

**Group your channels.** Click the arrow next to Channels, then **Manage → Edit All Sections**.
You can make sections and drag channels into them. Channels sort alphabetically inside a
section, but you can reorder the sections themselves.

**Color-code your workspaces.** Preferences → Themes. Light and dark applies everywhere, but
colors are per workspace, which makes it obvious at a glance which one you're looking at.

**Set reminders on posts.** If you want to deal with something later, use **More options** on
the post and set a reminder. Slackbot will DM you at the time you pick.
