# Activity: You're the Critic

**IGME-110 | Week TBD | Take-Home Activity**

> 🚧 **Not scheduled.** This activity exists and is ready to run, but has not been
> assigned a week for Fall 2026. It may not run at all.

Find a peer-reviewed article about games or interactive media, read it properly, and
critique it. Not summarize it. Critique it.

Then produce a correct APA citation for it, which you will need again, because P1 asks
for real sources and this is one.

## What you answer

Five questions about the article: what it claims, what evidence it brings, whether the
evidence supports the claim, what it leaves out, and whether you're convinced.

The last one is the only one with no wrong answer, and it's the one people skip.
