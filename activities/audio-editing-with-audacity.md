# Activity: Audio Editing with Audacity

**IGME-110 | Week 13A | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** Instructions are being lifted out of the Fall 2025 weekly notes.

P4 is a video ad, and the fastest way to make a video look amateur is bad audio. People
will forgive a shaky shot. They will not sit through clipping, hiss, or a voiceover
that's quieter than the music behind it.

Audacity is free, it runs on everything, and it does everything this project needs.

## What you'll practice

- Recording and importing, and what sample rate and bit depth actually mean
- Trimming, splitting, and moving clips on a timeline
- Levels: normalize, amplify, and why "make it louder" is usually the wrong fix
- Noise reduction on a real noisy recording
- Fades and crossfades
- Exporting something you can drop into a video editor
