# Activity: Best Foot Forward

**IGME-110 | Week 15B | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** Adapted from Fall 2025. Submission details not final.

What do people see when they look you up?

Employers will. Co-op coordinators will. So will the person deciding whether to put you
on a team. Knowing what's out there about you is worth an hour, and putting something
good out there deliberately is worth more.

## Part 1: Look yourself up

Search for yourself a few different ways: your full name, your name plus RIT, your
usernames, an image search. Use more than one search engine, and try it signed out.

You're looking for two things. What's there that you didn't put there, and what isn't
there that should be.

## Part 2: Build the thing you want found

Create or update a LinkedIn profile. You have four projects from this semester and that
is more than most first-years can point at. The video ad and the infographic in
particular are portfolio pieces right now, today, before you take another course.
