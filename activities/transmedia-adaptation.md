# Activity: Transmedia Adaptation

**IGME-110 | Week 5A | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** Adapted from Tony's Fall 2025 version. Instructions not yet lifted over.

A story told as a film is not the same story told as a game, a podcast, or a comic. The
medium is not a container you pour content into. It changes what the story can do.

This one is a round robin. Your group starts with something in one medium, adapts it to
another, hands it on, and the next group adapts that. By the end it has been through
several media and looks nothing like where it started. What survived the trip and what
didn't is the actual content of the class.

## What you'll practice

- Naming the affordances of a medium: what it's good at, what it can't do
- Deciding what is core to a story and what was just an artifact of its format
- Working fast on something disposable, which is a real skill
