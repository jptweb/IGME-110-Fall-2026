# Activity: Mood Board

**IGME-110 | Week 6A | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** Fall 2025 ran this from a Google Doc. Being rewritten for Fall 2026.

Before you make a visual design, whether it's print, web, or interactive, it helps to
build a mood board. A mood board expresses the feel and the design goals of a project
when you can't yet put them into words, which is most of the time at the start.

You are about to start P2, so this is not an abstract exercise. The board you make here
is the one your infographic should end up looking like.

## What you'll do

Collect color, type, imagery, and texture that communicate the feeling you want, arrange
them, and then write a short justification: what is this board claiming your project
should feel like, and why does that fit your audience?

The justification is the graded part. Anyone can collect pretty images.
