# Activity: Asset Scavenger Hunt

**IGME-110 | Week 4A | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** The Fall 2025 version of this ran as extra credit out of a Google Doc.
> Content is being rewritten for Fall 2026 and is not final.

You are going to need images, audio, fonts, and video for P2, P3, and P4. Most of what
you'd grab off a search results page you are not allowed to use, and "I found it on
Google" is not a license.

This is the hunt: find real, usable, correctly-attributed assets, and be able to prove
why you're allowed to use each one.

## What it covers

- Creative Commons licenses and what each one actually permits
- Public domain vs "free to view"
- Where the good sources are: openly-licensed image, audio, and font libraries
- Writing a correct attribution line

## Roughly what you'll do

Find one asset of each type, record its license, its source, and the attribution string
you'd use. The point is the proof, not the file.
