# Activity: Asset Scavenger Hunt

**IGME-110 | Week 4A | In-Class Activity | 10 points**

A picture is worth a thousand words. The wrong picture is worth several thousand more, and
those ones come from a lawyer.

You are about to need images, audio, and textures for Projects 2, 3, and 4. Most of what you
would grab off a search results page you are not allowed to use, and "I found it on Google" is
not a license. This is the hunt: find six real, usable, correctly attributed assets, and be
able to prove for each one why you are allowed to use it.

**We do this in class. It closes tonight.**

## What to do

Make a Google Doc titled `Asset Scavenger Hunt: [your name]`.

Find one asset for each of the six items below. For each one, record four things:

1. **The asset itself.** Paste the image in. For the audio item, paste the link.
2. **The source URL.** Where you got it, not where it appears on a search page.
3. **A link to the license**, showing it is free to use. Not a description of the license, a
   link to it.
4. **The attribution line** you would put in your project, written out. If the license does not
   require attribution, say which license and say that it doesn't.

Item 4 is the one people skip, and it's the one worth the most.

### The six

1. **A photo using roughly equal amounts of [RIT's official orange](https://www.rit.edu/brandportal/colors) and white.**
2. **A spritesheet with at least four sprites on it.**
3. **A grass texture that tiles seamlessly.** You can check with [this tool](https://www.pycheung.com/checker/).
4. **A painting from (not of) a Croatian museum.** Read that twice. The museum is where the
   painting lives, not what the painting shows.
5. **A black and white photograph of a street in the 1920s.**
6. **A music track you could legally put under a video ad you post publicly.** This one has a
   catch, and finding the catch is the point.

## What to hand in

Set your doc to **Anyone at RIT with the link, Viewer**, then hand the link in on myCourses
before the end of the day.

If you did not finish all six in class, hand in what you have. Getting four of them right is
worth more than six of them with the license fields empty.

## AI Expectations

**Do not use generative AI to make the assets.** The entire skill here is sourcing something
that already exists and proving you may use it, and a generated image skips past every part of
that.

Using AI to help you *search* is fine, and so is using it to check whether you have read a
license correctly. If you do, say so in a sentence at the top of your doc: which tool, and what
you used it for.

## Why this one matters later

Every project left in this course needs assets you did not make.

P2 is an infographic that needs images and probably icons. P3 needs interface elements. P4 is a
video ad that needs music, and the music item above is in here because that is the one that
catches people: a great deal of "free" audio is free for personal use only and forbids exactly
the thing you are about to do with it.

The habit of checking, and of writing the attribution down while you still remember where the
file came from, is the actual deliverable.
