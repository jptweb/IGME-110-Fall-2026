# Activity: Video Production

**IGME-110 | Week 13B | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** Adapted from Fall 2025. Asset links and submission details not final.

Make a short video that combines several clips into one coherent story.

**The audio is provided**, along with a transcript with time codes. You supply the
visuals. That's the whole shape of the exercise: you are cutting picture to a track that
already exists, which is exactly what P4 asks you to do.

You're responsible for finding or shooting the video you edit. Still images count if you
pan and zoom on them, and a good Ken Burns move often reads better than a bad clip.

## What you'll practice

- Cutting to a soundtrack, using the time codes to plan where cuts land
- Sequencing clips so they tell one story instead of being a pile of footage
- Pan and zoom on stills
- Transitions, and why you need far fewer than you think
- Exporting at a sane resolution and bitrate
