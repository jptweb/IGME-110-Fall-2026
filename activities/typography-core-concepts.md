# Activity: Typography Core Concepts

**IGME-110 | Week 6B | In-Class Activity**

> 🚧 **Draft.** Fall 2025 ran this from a Google Doc. Being rewritten for Fall 2026.

Type is the part of visual design you will use in every single project in this course
and most of the ones after it. It is also the part people fake their way through the
longest, because bad typography is invisible until someone points at it.

This one is technical on purpose: the vocabulary and the mechanics, not taste.

## What it covers

- Typeface vs font, and why the distinction matters when you're licensing one
- Serif, sans, mono, display, and what each is actually for
- Leading, tracking, kerning, and measure
- Hierarchy: making someone read things in the order you intended
- Pairing, and the rule that gets you 80% there
