# Activity: Paper Prototype of a Digital Game

**IGME-110 | Week 9A | In-Class Activity**

In groups, you'll take a video game and rebuild it as a paper prototype: a board or card game
made out of whatever is on the table. Paper is fast, cheap, and easy to throw away, which is the
whole point.

## What you'll do

1. **Get into groups of about four.** Your P1 team, your P3 team, or whoever is sitting near you.
2. **Pick a video game.** Ideally one everyone in your group knows, so nobody has to learn it first.
   Can't agree? Spin the [wheel of games](https://wheelofnames.com/tr5-h7j).
3. **Pick the core experience.** The one thing a player is actually there for, not every mechanic.
4. **Build it** with the paper, cards, dice, and odds and ends on the back table.

While you build, talk through: how fast does it play, what are players choosing between, what
should they feel, and what happens in play that you didn't design directly?

If you want a framework for that, look at [MDA](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/revisiting-the-mda-framework)
and [the eight kinds of fun](https://gnomestew.com/the-eight-types-of-fun/). Using AI to
brainstorm is fine if you say so. Expect to end up with something simpler than what it suggests.
