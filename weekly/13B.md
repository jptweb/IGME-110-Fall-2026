## A video file in five ideas

1. **A video is a stack of still images.** HD is 1920 by 1080 pixels per image. 4K is four
   times that.
2. **Frame rate is how many images per second.** Film is 24. TV and most web video is about
   30. Games aim for 60 or more, because you're steering.
3. **Uncompressed video is enormous.** One minute of plain HD is about 11 GB.
4. **A codec is the method that shrinks it.** H.264 is the common one. It gets that minute
   down to something like 60 MB, mostly by storing what changed between frames.
5. **A container is the box the result goes in.** `.mp4` and `.mov` are containers. The box
   holds a video track, audio tracks, and sometimes captions.

So when an assignment says "hand in an `.mp4`," it means: H.264 video in an MP4 container.
That's what the default export gives you in nearly every editor.
