## A good test task

Give the tester a reason and a goal, and don't say where to tap.

| | |
|---|---|
| ❌ Too vague | "Use the app." |
| ❌ Gives it away | "Tap Laundry, pick Gleason, then tap Hold." |
| ✅ | "Your laundry is piling up and you have class in an hour. Find a washer you can use right now and hold it." |

Under each task, write the screens you expect: *Home → Laundry → Gleason → Washer 4 → Held*. That's
for your team, not the tester.
