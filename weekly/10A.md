## In class

A short talk (a house with no blueprint, designs that trick people, three accessibility checks), then
we build a clickable phone app in Figma together. Don't get ahead, and put your hand up the moment
you're stuck.

The build follows **[this YouTube tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v1UKB-0EUhQ)**, with a
TazerBed app instead of hers. Missed class or fell behind? Watch it, or use the
**[step-by-step guide](../documents/teaching-guide-figma.md)**.

**[→ Activity: Enhance the Figma Prototype](../activities/enhance-the-figma-prototype.md)** · graded · due Thu Oct 29

Make it yours by Thursday night: three pages, something that scrolls, and one thing we didn't build
together.

## Before you add color

- Is "no" as easy to tap as "yes"? Is leaving as easy as joining?
- Don't rely on color alone, keep text easy to read, and make buttons big enough to tap.
