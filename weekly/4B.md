## III. Markdown, Live

Everything you've read in this course has been markdown. Every weekly note, including this one,
is a plain text file with a few symbols in it.

I'll type it in front of you with the preview open beside it. Eight things:

```
# heading         ## subheading        ---
- bullet          1. numbered
**bold**          *italic*
[text](url)       ![alt](image-url)
```

That's most of it. There's more, but you won't need it today, and the activity has three
reference links for when you do.

**Commit means save.** Every commit is a point you can go back to. That's the entire reason
version control exists, and it's why nobody in this field has a folder containing
`final_v2_FINAL_real.psd`.

## IV. The Activity

**[→ Activity: Markdown and GitHub](../activities/markdown-and-github.md)** · 10 points · due
tonight

Three parts:

1. **Make a repository** called `IGME-110-Repo`. Public, with a README.
2. **Practice in the README.** Headings, lists, links, an image, bold and italic. It's yours and
   nothing depends on it, so break it.
3. **Make a listicle.** A new file called `my-listicle.md`: five links on a theme you actually
   care about, two images, five emoji, real headings.

**Hand in the URL of your listicle file.** Copy it from the address bar. There is nothing to
upload.
