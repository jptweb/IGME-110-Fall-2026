**Tuesday, November 3**

## Your partner team

On Thursday you test with one other team. **[Find your pair on the teams page.](../documents/p3-teams.md)**
The team listed first has its prototype tested first, then you swap.

## In class: rehearse

Run your whole [test script](../documents/p3-test-script.md) once, start to finish, inside your team.

- Your **Computer** stays on Computer the whole time. That's the job that needs the practice.
- The other two take turns: one plays the tester and one reads the task. Don't play tester on a task
  you wrote.
- Write down everything that breaks.

Look for:

- A tap with no screen behind it
- The Computer hunting for the next sheet
- A task that tells the tester where to tap
- Anyone reading the expected path out loud

If your rehearsal finds nothing, run it again with a tougher tester. Then spend the rest of class
fixing what it found.

## Get your script ready for Thursday

- **Put your roles on the script:** Facilitator, Computer, Scribe.
- **There's a new line in the template:** *Had this tester seen our screens before today? Yes / No /

- **I'll print three copies of your script** from the PDF you hand in Wednesday, one for each

- **Screens in Figma?** Tell me before you leave today and I'll print them.

## Bonus point (optional)

Run one real test of your script with someone who has never seen your screens. Two ways to do it:

- **In class, today.** Your tester has to be someone who is **not on your team and not on your
  Thursday partner team**. Check the [teams page](../documents/p3-teams.md) to see who's who.
  You don't have the printout yet, but you have the questions, so write down the tester's answer to
  each one on paper or in a doc. Then raise your hand and show me, and I'll note your bonus point.
- **Outside class, before you hand in Wednesday.** Run it with a real person: a roommate, a friend,
  anyone who hasn't seen your app. Fill in the script, and take a photo of you running the test with
  them, so I know it was a real person and not made-up answers. Add the photo as the last page of
  your Wednesday PDF.

It's a bonus, so if you're not sure you can do it for real, skip it.

## How Thursday works

Your partner team tests your prototype, one person at a time, while your team runs it. Get through
at least two testers. If there's time, the third person tests too. Then you swap and test theirs the
same way.

If you're on the other team and it isn't your turn, turn around or step away. At the very least,
don't look at the papers. A tester who has already seen the screens can't tell you anything new.

in 2011, but the method hasn't changed.
