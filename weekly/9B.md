### Sample persona

This one is for a made-up product, the TaserBed™, an app for an alarm bed that shocks you awake. Yours goes on its own page with a photo, and should come out of your Project 1 research.

> **Chad, 32** *(photo here)*
>
> Consultant at a big firm. Single, lives alone downtown, works 60-hour weeks and travels Monday to Thursday.
>
> **Goals:** Get up at 5:00 without hitting snooze. Control the bed from his phone while he's on the road.
>
> **Frustrations:** Sleeps through normal alarms. Every smart-home app he owns wants a different login.
>
> **Quote:** "If I hit snooze one more time, I'm going to miss another flight."
>
> **When he'd use it:** Sunday night he sets the whole week's wake-up times and shock level in one go. On Wednesday at the airport, a meeting moves earlier and he bumps Thursday's alarm up from his phone.
