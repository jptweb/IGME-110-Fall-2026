**Thursday, October 22**

> **Today in one line:** A quick walk through the Work Plan, then the rest of class with your Project 3 team

## In class

We'll start by going over what the [Work Plan and Personas](../documents/p3-prototype.md#1-work-plan-and-personas) asks for. After that, the time is yours. Sit with your team ([find it here](../documents/p3-teams.md)) and get as much done in the room as you can.

Try to leave with:

- Your product picked from one of the Project 1 proposals, and why
- Three to five features, with the ones your prototype will show marked
- A short paragraph on who it's for
- Your [roles](../documents/p3-prototype.md#your-three-roles), and who owns which persona
- When you'll meet, where you'll talk, and where your files will live

## Over the weekend

Each person makes their own persona, one page each. Check [what a persona needs](../documents/p3-prototype.md#what-a-persona-needs) before you start. Then one person puts everything into a single PDF and submits it for the team by Sunday night.
