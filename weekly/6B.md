## II. Typography Core Concepts

**[→ Activity: Typography Core Concepts](../activities/typography-core-concepts.md)** · 15 points ·
hand in by the end of the day

Three parts:

1. **Anatomy of a typeface:** the names for the parts of a letter, and what changes from one
   typeface to the next.
2. **Digital type files:** what a font family actually is, and why some fonts are missing letters.
3. **Anti-aliasing:** how a curved letter ends up on a screen made of square pixels. This part is
   in Photoshop.

Study Guide 6 covered picking and pairing fonts. This is the other half: what a font is and how it
gets onto a screen. You'll use the vocabulary on your infographic, and again in every visual
project after it.
