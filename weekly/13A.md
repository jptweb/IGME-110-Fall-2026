# Week 13A: Demo Day

**Tuesday, November 17**

> **Today in one line:** Try every team's prototype and vote for the one you'd actually use
> **Bring:** a charged laptop with your prototype open

## In class

1. **Set up.** Laptop open, prototype on the first screen.
2. **Host and explore.** One or two people stay with your prototype while the rest of your team
   tries everyone else's. Halfway through, swap.
3. **Vote.** Rate each team you tried from 1 to 5 and pick one favorite (not your own team). Hand
   in your ballot before you leave. Your vote is anonymous, and the winner in each section gets a
   prize, announced Thursday.

Every team's prototype is linked on the
**[Project 4: Available Prototypes](../documents/p4-prototypes.md)** page.
