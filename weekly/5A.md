## II. Transmedia Adaptation

**[→ Activity: Transmedia Adaptation](../activities/transmedia-adaptation.md)** · 10 points · write-up
due tonight

Study Guide 5 was about how the medium changes the message. Today you test it on your own story.

Your team writes a very short story, and it travels around the room in an envelope. The next team
turns it into a comic, the team after that into a choose-your-own-adventure, and the one after that
back into a story. Then it comes home, and you get to see what's left.

It's pencil and paper, and it moves fast: four rounds, ten minutes each. The activity page has an
example and explains how the handoff works.

There's one thing to hand in: each of you writes a short reflection and hands it in on myCourses.
Photograph your sheets before you go, since I collect the envelopes and those photos are what
you'll be writing from. Posting them in Slack is optional, and it's worth doing because the drift
is funnier across eight stories than one.
