# Project 3: Test Script

**→ [Back to Project 3](p3-prototype.md#2-lo-fi-prototype-and-test-script)**

Copy everything below the line into a Google Doc and fill in your tasks before deliverable 2 is
due. On test day, make **one copy per tester**. The Scribe fills it in while the test runs.

Your team's job on test day is to watch, not to help. When a tester gets stuck, the Facilitator can
ask "What would you do here?" or "What are you looking for?" and then waits. Getting stuck is what
you came to find.

This kind of testing is qualitative. You're collecting what people do and say, not scores, so the
notes about where someone hesitated matter more than the numbers.
