Copy everything below the line into a Google Doc. **Fill in the parts marked ✏️ before Wednesday.**
Everything else stays as written. On test day, make **one copy per tester**, and the Scribe fills in
"What happened" while the test runs.

Your team's job on test day is to watch, not to help. When a tester gets stuck, the Facilitator can
ask "What would you do here?" and then waits. Getting stuck is what you came to find.

You're collecting what people do and say, so write down where someone hesitated and what they
said. That's what your Testing Results will be built from.
