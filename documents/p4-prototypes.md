**A few things worth knowing before you pick:**

- Open the write-up first. It has the personas and the user testing results, which is
  most of what you need for [part 2 of your work plan](p4-video-ad.md#1-work-plan).
- You're selling the product, not the prototype. You don't need every screen to be finished.
- If a link doesn't open for you, tell me on Slack rather than picking something else. It's a
  sharing setting, and it takes about ten seconds to fix.
