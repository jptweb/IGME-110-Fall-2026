**IGME-110 | Week 1B**

About four minutes, done in class. It is the only thing I use to build your Project 1 team.

## Take the one for your section

There are two forms. Teams are built inside your own section, so the wrong form puts you on
nobody's list.

- **Section 01** (Tue/Thu 2:00 to 3:15): [SECTION 01 LINK GOES HERE]
- **Section 07** (Tue/Thu 3:30 to 4:45): [SECTION 07 LINK GOES HERE]

We'll open it together in class on Thursday. It closes Friday night.

## Why I'm asking

Group projects usually go badly for a boring reason: four people cannot find one hour a week when
all of them are free. That is fixable, and week one is when it's fixable, before anyone is on a
team.

So the schedule question is the one that matters most. The rest is secondary.

## What's on it

1. Your name and RIT email
2. When you're free to meet outside class
3. A few questions about what you've done before and how you like to work
4. Whether there's anyone you'd like to work with, or would rather not
5. Anything you want me to know

## Answer the schedule question honestly

It's a grid: days across, rough time blocks down. Check the blocks when you could realistically
meet, not every block when you're technically not in class.

If you check everything, you will end up on a team that meets Sunday at 8 AM. Include weekends if
weekends genuinely work, since that's often where the only overlap is.

## The teammate questions

You can name up to two people you'd like to work with, and up to two you'd rather not. A few
things worth knowing about how I use them:

- **They're requests, not guarantees.** I try to honor them, and most of the time I can. Schedule
  overlap comes first.
- **Nobody sees your answers but me.** That goes for both lists, and you don't owe me a reason for
  either one.
- **Leaving them blank is normal.** It's the first week and most of you have met maybe three
  people. Blank costs you nothing.

If you meet someone this week you'd want on your team, remember their name. That's the whole prep
for this question.

## The AI question

One question asks how you feel about working with teammates who use AI tools. It's there because
that's something teams genuinely disagree about, and it is much easier to sort out in week one
than in week four.

There's no right answer and it isn't a test. I am not building AI-only teams or AI-free teams. The
course AI policy is set per assignment and this doesn't change it: using AI where the assignment
allows it is fine, hiding it is not.

## The last question is read by a person

I read every answer to the last question myself, and nobody else sees them.

If something affects when you can meet or how you work, tell me there. Athletics travel, a job with
set shifts, a long commute, an accommodation, anything at all. You don't have to explain more than
you want to.

## What happens next

Over the weekend I run the answers through a tool called gruepr. It builds teams that actually
share free time, and spreads experience around so no team is short on any one skill.

Teams get posted Monday. You meet them Tuesday in class.
