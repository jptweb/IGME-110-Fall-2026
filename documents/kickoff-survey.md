# Course Kickoff Survey

**IGME-110 | Week 1B**

Two minutes, done in class. It is the only thing I use to build your Project 1 team.

## Why I'm asking

Group projects usually go badly for a boring reason: four people cannot find one hour a week when
all of them are free. That is fixable, and week one is when it's fixable, before anyone is on a
team.

So the schedule question is the one that matters. The rest of the form is secondary.

## What's on it

1. Your name and RIT username
2. Which section you're in
3. When you're generally free to meet outside class
4. Which of a few skills you've done before
5. Anything you want me to know

## Answer question 3 honestly

Question 3 is a grid: days across, rough time blocks down. Check the blocks when you could
realistically meet, not every block when you're technically not in class.

If you check everything, you will end up on a team that meets Sunday at 8 AM. Include weekends if
weekends genuinely work, since that's often where the only overlap is.

## Question 5 is read by a person

I read every answer to the last question myself, and nobody else sees them.

If something affects when you can meet or how you work, tell me there. Athletics travel, a job with
set shifts, a long commute, an accommodation, anything at all. You don't have to explain more than
you want to.

## What happens next

Over the weekend I run the answers through a tool called gruepr. It builds teams that actually
share free time, and spreads experience around so no team is short on any one skill.

Teams get posted Monday. You meet them Tuesday in class.
