# IGME-110 Fall 2026: Tentative Schedule

> **This schedule is tentative.** The topics and the order they come in are settled. Individual
> activities, and sometimes a deadline, can shift as the semester goes.
>
> **myCourses is the source of truth for deadlines.** If this page and myCourses disagree,
> myCourses is right and I want to know about it.

Two sections, both in GOL 2000: section 01 at 2:00 and section 07 at 3:30, Tuesdays and Thursdays.

The semester runs in four blocks, one per project. They build on each other. You research a
product, explain it visually, prototype it, then sell it.

## The weekly rhythm

Most of the deadlines in this course repeat, so they are worth learning once rather than looking
up fifteen times.

- **Study guides are due Monday**, before the week they set up. Not every week has one.
- **In-class activities close the night we do them.** Most class sessions have one.
- **Project deliverables are due Friday.** The table below has the exceptions, and there are two.

## Dates that change the schedule

- **Sep 7 is Labor Day.** We meet Tuesday and Thursday, so no class is lost. The week 3 study guide
  shifts to Tuesday at 2:00 rather than being due on a holiday.
- **Oct 12 and 13 are October Break.** No Tuesday class in week 8.
- **Nov 25 through 27 is Thanksgiving.** Tuesday Nov 24 is a normal class day. No Thursday class in
  week 14.
- **Dec 7 is the last day of classes.** Finals run Dec 9 through 16.
